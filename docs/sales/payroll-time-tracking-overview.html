1. The crew's day clockShop to shop, not stop to stop

  • At the top of My Schedule in the crew app, whoever has the phone taps Start Day. It starts the day for everyone on the crew's roster that day. The card then reads "Day started 7:02 AM, 3 on the clock."
  • End Day clocks the whole crew out at once. If a job is still running, the app asks for confirmation first.
  • The roster comes from your crew assignments, adjusted by any same-day move on the Crew Board. Anyone marked Not working is skipped.
  • Days follow your company's time zone, not the phone's, so a shift always lands on the right day.

2. Real-life situations, handledLate arrivals, early departures, and forgotten taps

Edit Crew (in the crew app)
  • Not working: sick or off today, not clocked in by mistake
  • Clock In: arrived late, shift starts now
  • Left for the day: ends their shift and any open job punch
  • Back: returns the same day; time away is taken off as an unpaid break
When someone forgets
  • Starting the first job opens the day anyway, so payroll always has a shift
  • Those days are flagged Started at first job so the office can correct the real start
  • Breaks use Take a Break / Resume; a break left open at clock-out is closed and still subtracted
  • Overnight and storm shifts that cross midnight work correctly

3. Paid time and job time, kept separate on purposeDrive time and shop time are paid, but they aren't on a job

ClockWhat it drives
Start Day / End DayPayroll. Each person's paid day: clock-in at the shop to clock-out, less lunch and unpaid pauses.
Start Job / Stop JobJob Costing. Per-person punches on each stop, so labor cost per job stays accurate.

The Payroll report shows both side by side. The gap between paid hours and on-job hours is your drive and shop time.

4. The Payroll tabReport Center → Payroll

FeatureDetails
Pick the periodFrom/To dates, or This week, Last week, Last 2 weeks (Monday to Sunday). Up to 62 days in one report.
Totals by personOne row per employee: Day hours, On jobs, Extra, and Total. Click a row to see day-by-day detail.
Correct any dayChange clock-in/out times, unpaid break minutes, or type the hours directly. Add Extra hours (shop time, PTO) and a note saying why. An end time earlier than the start is read as the next morning. A reset arrow restores the recorded figures; edited days show an adjusted badge.
Needs-a-look flagsAn amber badge appears when a shift was never ended, a job punch is still open, or no one started the day. Expand the person to see which day.
ExportExcel (Totals sheet and Daily sheet), CSV of totals by employee, CSV of daily detail, or PDF. Hours export as numbers so payroll uploads can add them up.
Corrections stay on the Payroll report. The recorded shifts and punches are never rewritten, and Job Costing, job hours, and the Timesheet report are not affected by a payroll correction.

5. Control and accessPayroll is sensitive, so it's locked down

Who sees it
  • Admins, and roles granted View & Edit Payroll Report
  • Everyone else doesn't see the tab
  • Crew logins can't read it at all, enforced in the database as well as the app
Who clocks the crew
  • The day clock is for the crew leader's login, for that crew's own roster only
  • Office staff fix anything after the fact in Payroll or with Edit Job Times on the Dispatch Board
